### Design specificity verdict

The typography and colour system are competent, but the *page* is not. Read the skeleton without reading a word: sticky glass nav with a filled pill CTA -> 92vh full-bleed photograph with a display lockup and two buttons -> full-bleed solid-colour band with one sentence -> 3-up card grid -> 3-up horizontal row list -> 3-up figure grid -> 2-column CTA block -> 3-column near-black footer. That is the canonical 2020 product-landing template with the serial numbers filed off. Six of the seven sections are "heading + exactly three items in a three-column grid" at identical gaps, identical head margins (`.section-head{margin-bottom:3rem}`, line 299) and identical media radii. A coffee roaster or a B2B SaaS could run this file with the words swapped and nobody would notice the seam.

What is genuinely authored for this product is almost entirely verbal. "Lo que te ponés después" (580), "Lo que queda en la pared" (645), "Quedarse cuando el resto ya se fue. El after, el frío, el grupo." (614), "El tackle no es el final. Es el principio de levantarse." (711) — the best copy on the page and the one asset that could not be moved. The structural layer contributes nothing: no scoreboard, no pitch stripe, no try-line rule, no jersey number, no size chart, no line-out motif anywhere in the layout. The rugby lives in the adjectives, not in the composition. A brand whose thesis is *el carácter se forja* has an entire visual vocabulary of scrums, tackles and floodlit grass available and uses none of it.

Category-interchangeable choices are specific and numerous. `Archivo Black` + `Archivo` is a neo-grotesque pairing; heritage rugby is a serif/collegiate language, so the type is closer to a streetwear label than to a fifteen-a-side club. The three lookbook photographs (702-739) are Unsplash stock of generic players, and the actual product is not identifiable in any of them, while all three product 4:5 shots are flat-lay/ghost images. **The product never appears on a person anywhere on this page.** For a clothing brand that is the single largest missed opportunity on the surface. Then there is what is missing on the commerce side entirely: no sizes, no colours, no fit notes, no stock state, no shipping, no returns, no payment methods. And the two product halves are split across two sections with two different visual systems (4:5 cards vs 92px squares) while the nav exposes only one of them.

One unresolved brand question sits underneath all of it: the *rendered* name is "Temple" everywhere a human sees it (8, 559, 530-533, 798), but "Temple **XV**" appears in three `alt` attributes (22, 27, 553), in the hero filenames, in the project directory `templexv`, and in the hostname `t1mpl2xv.pages.dev`. Nothing rendered ever says XV. A brand's first emotional beat disagrees between the page and its own social card.

### Strengths

1. **The media layer is genuinely professional.** `.media{aspect-ratio}` + absolutely-positioned `object-fit:cover` img (317-327) means zero layout shift; every declared `width`/`height` matches its real file (840×1050 = 4:5 vs `aspect-ratio:4/5`; 840×1120 = 3:4 vs `3/4`). `fetchpriority="high"` on the hero only, `loading="lazy"` on all nine below-fold images, and `sizes` that track the grid. And lines 459-462 are a Spanish comment explaining that `clip-path` on the *observed* element makes Chrome report intersection ratio 0, so the threshold is never reached — a documented, correctly-fixed IntersectionObserver bug. This is the most senior part of the file.
2. **The copy is the brand.** Every description is a use scene, not a feature list. Zero occurrences of *premium*, *calidad*, *pasión*, *diseño*, *estilo*. The voseo is idiomatic throughout. This is what a category-specific design looks like, and it is entirely in the words.
3. **Restraint and token discipline on colour and motion.** One accent, redefined completely for dark mode including `--glass`, `--on-accent` and all four `--footer-*` tokens — nothing leaks a hardcoded light value except the two intentional `#e8d5c4` hero values. Worst measured text pair in the system is `--ink-dim` on `--surface` at **4.94:1**, all AA. `role="list"` on all six `<ul>`s. A genuinely comprehensive `prefers-reduced-motion` block (511-520).

### Priority issues

**[P0] Every WhatsApp link has no destination phone number**

- **Why it matters:** All ten conversion links (539, 566, 585, 603, 621, 650, 663, 676, 756, 791) are `https://wa.me/?text=…` with no number. That form opens WhatsApp's contact picker with the text staged, not a chat with Temple. Verified by request: it 302s to `api.whatsapp.com/send/?text=…&type=custom_url`, while `wa.me/<number>?text=…` produces `&phone=<number>&type=phone_number`. The entire business model of this site is these ten links, and 100% of them require the user to find and select "Temple" at the highest-intent moment on the page. This is where the conversion dies. Compounding it, `connect-src 'self' https://wa.me` in `_headers:7` protects a threat that cannot occur — link navigation is never governed by `connect-src`.
- **Fix:** `https://wa.me/54XXXXXXXXX?text=…` on all ten, de-duplicated into one `data-wa` attribute plus a handler so the number lives in exactly one place. Make the prefill carry the two decisions the page failed to collect: `?text=Hola%20Temple%2C%20quiero%20la%20Campera%20-%20talle%20M%20-color%20negro`.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`

**[P1] There is no mobile navigation — three of five destinations are unreachable on the most likely device**

- **Why it matters:** `.nav-links{display:none}` at line 502 removes Colección, Lookbook and Contacto with **no replacement** — verified: **0 `<button>` elements in the file**, no hamburger, no `<details>`, no scroller. A visitor from `instagram.com/temple.argentina` on a phone must scroll five screens or use browser find. The 640px threshold is also crossed at **200% zoom on a 1280px display** (WCAG 1.4.4). Meanwhile the scroll-spy (822-836) keeps updating three links the user cannot see, and `#accesorios` is absent from the nav at every width even though it sells three of six products.
- **Fix:** `<button class="nav-toggle" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="nav-links">` inside `.nav-inner`, toggling `data-open` on `.nav`; the panel inherits the existing `--glass` and `backdrop-filter` so it needs no new visual language. Add a `#accesorios` nav item so the scroll-spy stops lying. Minimum acceptable fallback if a menu is out of scope: replace `display:none` with `overflow-x:auto;white-space:nowrap` — never hide navigation without putting something in its place.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable adapt`

**[P1] The commerce-critical information does not exist, the purchase ask is unaccompanied, and the hero inverts its own hierarchy**

- **Why it matters:** No sizes, no colours, no fit notes, no stock, no shipping coverage, no returns, no payment methods, no instalments. At $89.000–$168.000 with no card form, every real objection is unanswered at the exact moment intent peaks. - **Fix:** Swap the classes at 565/566 so the WhatsApp action carries `.btn-cream`. Add a `.product-sizes` line (S M L XL) inside each `.product-body` above the CTA. Add a three-item reassurance row in `#contacto` (envíos a todo el país / Mercado Pago o transferencia / cambio por talle) built from existing tokens. Add a "Cómo comprar" footer column with `© 2026`.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`

**[P1] One parse error permanently hides all nine product images, and the reveal has no safety net**

- **Why it matters:** `clip-path:inset(0 0 100% 0)` is gated on `.js` being added unconditionally and immediately at line 6 in `<head>`, but the class that removes the clip (`.is-in`) is added only by the IIFE at 804-838. If that IIFE fails to parse or throws before line 813, `.js` is present, `.is-in` never arrives, and **all nine product, accessory and lookbook images stay at zero height and invisible for the life of the page** — a single-page catalogue showing no product imagery. There is no `<noscript>`, no `@media (scripting:none)` escape, no timeout. The comment at 459-462 shows the author already debugged one version of this failure mode. Related: no `onerror` anywhere, so a failed product image leaves a flat `--surface` rectangle forever with `alt=""` announcing nothing.
- **Fix:** Move `document.documentElement.classList.add('js')` from line 6 into the first line of the IIFE at 805, so any failure leaves `.js` unset and every image visible. Add `@media (scripting:none){.reveal img{clip-path:none}}` and a 3s `setTimeout` that force-reveals anything still hidden.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`

**[P2] The footer is a redundant copy of the page, so the journey ends on a repeat**

Column one (775) restates the hero verbatim; column two (779-786) is a six-item index whose six labels resolve to just two anchors (the product cards carry no `id`); column three duplicates the section directly above. `.footer-legal` offers "Temple · Argentina" and the band sentence — no year, no terms, no privacy. ### Minor observations

- **17 distinct font sizes**, six of them inside a 0.25rem band (0.95, 1.02, 1.05, 1.1, 1.18, 1.2rem) that read as perceptually identical. A 1.25 ratio would collapse the problem.
- **Only 4 type sizes are fluid.** `.wordmark` is a 3-step hard function — 6rem → 4.75rem at 1100px → 3.5rem at 700px — so between 700 and 1100px the six letters stay locked at 76px while `justify-content:space-between` (237) spreads them further apart as the viewport narrows. The loosest state of the wordmark is the mid-tablet one.
- `.access:hover{background:var(--base)}` (369) shifts `#f0ede8` → `#faf9f6`, ~1.3% luminance. The only interactive element on the page whose hover you cannot see, and there is no `.access:hover .media img` transform (unlike 328).
- Four radii with no tokens: 16px on media/product/access, `.5rem` on btn, `.45rem` on cta/skip, 9px on the mark — and 16px is the only radius in `px` in a rem-based system, so it cannot respond to root font-size.
- `.btn-cream` (202-203) hardcodes `#faf9f6`/`#141311` instead of `--base`/`--ink` — *required*, since those tokens invert in dark mode — and its hover `#e8d5c4` is the same literal as `.tagline` (256) and the hero scrim gradient (224). Three shared values, no token.
- `lang="es"` (2) should be `es-AR` given the voseo copy, while `og:locale` (14) already declares `es_AR`. Document and social card disagree.
- No print stylesheet: since `.js .reveal img` relies on JS to lift the clip, printing without JS gives nine fully clipped images. The `.js` gate is correct; the print path is unhandled.
- `role="list"` on 7 lists is redundant now that both Chrome (2023) and Safari (2024) fixed the `list-style:none` semantics bug, and risks doubled announcements in older VoiceOver + Safari. **Verify with a real screen reader before removing.**
- `aria-current="true"` should be `"location"` for a same-page section link.
- `temple-mark.png` is 256×256 / 30.7KB rendered at 34×34 with no `srcset` — 7.5× oversampled, 28KB of waste above the fold on every load.
- Six invalid nestings: `<h3>` inside `<span class="product-body">` / `<span class="access-body">` (594-595, 612-613, 630-631, 655-656, 668-669, 681-682). A `<span>`'s content model is phrasing content; `<h3>` is flow. It renders as authored and does **not** break the a11y tree, but it fails validation and will bite on any re-serialisation.
- `.product-price` in `--ink` at 1.05rem (339) vs `.access-price` in `--accent` at 0.95rem (377): two visual languages for the same fact.
- `--accent` as a footer hover underline (448) is **1.85:1** on `--footer-bg` in light mode — effectively invisible. A page-surface accent used on the one inverted surface in the system.
- The reveal stagger is hard-coded to `:nth-child(2)`/`:nth-child(3)` (468-473), so a fourth product silently gets no delay.
- `min-height:92vh;min-height:92svh;` (217-218) has no `@supports` guard, so the `vh` line is unreachable. The dark block re-declares `--footer-ink` and `--footer-dim` byte-identically (79-80) — dead overrides.
- `scroll-margin-top:4.5rem` (219, 297) is never overridden at the 640px breakpoint where the nav actually shrinks, so the offset stops tracking the real bar.

### Questions to consider

1. **The product never appears on a person.** The hero is a rugby ball at night, the lookbook is three Unsplash photographs of generic players, and the three product shots are flat-lays or ghosts. Not one image on this page shows a human wearing Temple. Everything about the *structure* is interchangeable and everything about the *words* is not — which means the one asset class that would make this layout impossible to reuse for a café is the one that is missing. What is stopping you from shooting six real people in real gear on a real pitch, and would that one decision change more than any layout work on this page?
2. **Is the brand "Temple" or "Temple XV"?** Everything a human sees says Temple; everything behind it says Temple XV. A brand's name is its first emotional beat and it currently disagrees between the page and its own social card. Resolve this before anything else, because every naming decision downstream inherits the answer.
3. **The `wordmark` spreading TEMPLE to the full container width (236-242) is the only spatially-authored gesture in the file** — and the only moment a café could not lift verbatim. It is also a one-off. Why is that single idea carrying the entire page when the brand has a whole visual vocabulary available? What does the layout look like if that gesture gets one more move instead of the band being 60% empty?
4. **The pre-filled WhatsApp message is the only shared state between this page and the sale, and it throws away everything the user just read.** What if the product card captured talle and color and the link shipped them — turning a cold "quiero la campera" into a structured order the seller can fulfil without one round-trip?
